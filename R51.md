## Contemporaneous alternatives outside East Asia

**England NHS, 2009 within the 2005–2014 linkage.** Martin and colleagues analyzed **5,093,615** singleton births in NHS maternity units across the ten years. Their Methods describe observed hour and minute in birth notifications, actual birth dates and infant sex in the linked material. Published analysis uses hourly groups. The authors describe restricted data access through the ONS Secure Research Service and required permissions. R51 has no access approval or confirmation of usable birth-unit geography in a 2009 extract for this use. This is the clearest documented historical minute-level alternative reviewed here outside East Asia. The published ten-year analysis count is distinct from any eligible 2009 frame for R51. [Original study, Methods and Data Availability](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0198183) · [ONS project accreditation](https://www.ons.gov.uk/aboutus/whatwedo/statistics/requestingstatistics/secureresearchservice/applyforanaccreditedresearchproject)

**Colombia DANE, standard 1999 and 2009 layouts.** The official HTML dictionaries and publisher DDI/XML exports agree in order on all **33** and **38** variables, respectively. The infant's birth fields include year, month, sex and occurrence municipality. The similarly named `FECHA_NACM` refers to the prior live-born child. The reviewed layouts lack the current infant's day of birth and clock. Neither layout can supply the registered comparison. [1999 layout](https://microdatos.dane.gov.co/index.php/catalog/366/data-dictionary/F11?file_name=Nacimientos_1999) · [2009 layout](https://microdatos.dane.gov.co/index.php/catalog/375/data-dictionary/F32?file_name=nac2009) · [Field-by-field review](rounds/r51/dane_dictionary_review.json)

## Reopening criteria

For an East Asian transfer test, first qualify the **HA 2009** route or a documented **1999 or 2009 Zhejiang** custodian frame. For each, obtain a dated field specification tying actual local birth date and minute, recorded infant sex and actual occurrence location to one infant. Ask the custodian to produce permitted coverage and missingness counts by year and geographic frame, with checks for rounded clocks, facility mapping, duplicates and actual birth versus certificate issue or filing. A single hospital or part-year sample supports a claim about that frame. Where data must remain in place, confirm that the custodian approves the specified calculation and can release disclosure-approved statistics.

Only an authorized frame supports a cohort registration and external scoring. Prespecify viable precision, sampling, geography and timezone handling before scoring. Keep the four models, Brazil 2006 reference, age-34 ten-year window, P75 and +5 percentage-point decision. The available forms, dictionaries and aggregates did not establish a joint distribution adequate for a new R51 identification bound. R47's partial-information result concerns its own P80 panels.
