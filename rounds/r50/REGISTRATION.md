# R50 registration for external study readiness

[中文](REGISTRATION.zh.md) · [Research program](../../docs/RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md)

R50 determines whether a contemporaneous external birth source can test the R49 result. The registered R49 model, Brazil 2006 reference and +5 percentage-point threshold are fixed. The project has seen the R47 aggregate bounds and published R49 effects. It has not inspected new external model scores. Verify permissions and source fields before acquiring or scoring individual research records.

## Sources and decision

Start with the Japanese perinatal registry and standard controlled Japanese vital records identified in R47. Check actual years of births, release fields, location representation and conditions for the specific use. Check the US NCHS 1999 and 2009 public natality record layouts and documented access routes as an outside-East-Asia alternative. A qualifying frame has the actual birth year, full local birth date, minutes, recorded sex and usable occurrence location on the same record. Verify record grain, sample selection, overlaps with prior data, address precision and authorization. A registration form, research paper or field dictionary confirms only what it documents. [R47 source assessment](../r47/SOURCES.zh.md)

If one qualifying year becomes available for this use, register its comparison as a separate external cohort. Prefer 1999 or 2009. For one cohort, the main replication window starts at age 34, as motivated by R49. Use the same 75th-percentile rule, 40 annual observations at completed ages 20–59 and 31 possible starts of a ten-year window. The cohort effect is the mean of its record-centered age-34 gap indicator minus the fixed Brazil 2006 reference mean. If the interval lies wholly below or above +5 points, report exclusion or support for that cohort. A threshold-crossing interval is unresolved. Freeze the sampling and uncertainty details before target scoring. Full-age and multi-cohort comparisons need their own prespecified selection correction.

When only marginal counts are available, use documented identification bounds if there is adequate information to compute them. R47's published bounds belong to its own panels and percentile. Absent suitable fields, access or attainable precision, give a traceable no-go decision and reopening conditions. An absence of access leaves the external scientific result unresolved.

## Computation where data are held

Check whether an authorized custodian could compute the fixed-age contrast without exporting individual records. Compress the fixed R49 reference to four percentile cutoff rules per system across the four age bands and exact offsets from the Brazil 2006 reference. Verify the comparison operators at cutoffs and numeric neighbors against the integer-rank calculation. Test all stored R49 scores and reference scores with both implementations before releasing the cutoff table.

For age 34, encode each centered gap as an integer by multiplying by 31. For each authorized sampling stratum, the eligible count, sampled count, sum and sum of squares suffice for the weighted mean and the finite-population design variance of this fixed comparison. Verify the calculation against the group estimates and standard errors already published for R49, and check the variance on enumerated finite populations. Use only approved, disclosure-safe aggregates. This receipt assesses method transfer, with external validation contingent on an authorized and admissible new frame.
