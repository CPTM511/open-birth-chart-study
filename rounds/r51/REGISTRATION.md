# R51 registration for a contemporaneous external cohort

[简体中文](REGISTRATION.zh.md) · [Research program](../../docs/RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md) · [R50 feasibility result](../../R50.md)

R51 asks whether an independent birth archive outside Brazil and Mexico can determine which side of +5 percentage points its fixed age-34 P75 contrast falls on. R49 and R50 are seen evidence. Select all eligible source and design details before viewing external scores.

## Source search and admission

Prioritize 1999 and 2009 births in East Asian archives outside Japan. Look for dated Korean vital-record releases, Taiwan birth registry routes, Hong Kong registries and cohort materials, and mainland Chinese municipal archives or documented research extracts. Use official layouts, original studies and actual terms of use to distinguish collection fields, released fields and permissions for this study. Look at plausible contemporaneous alternatives outside East Asia if those routes do not qualify. Other years can support a narrower, year-specific study.

A candidate must have local date of actual birth, observed local hour and minute, recorded infant sex and a verifiable birth occurrence location on the same record. Establish one record per infant, eligible frame coverage, missing and invalid data, birth versus filing dates, source precision, reproducible timezone and geographic mapping, and disjointness from all prior source records. Document the applicable public release terms or written custodian approval for this purpose. Confirm achievable sampling precision from the eligible frame and justified variance assumptions. A custodian may keep the records in place and return permitted aggregates, as checked in R50.

## Enrollment and decision

Publish a separate cohort registration once a source qualifies and before scoring. Lock the frame, primary estimator, sampling and exclusions, interval construction and controls. Use the same four systems, 20–59 completed ages, the P75 rules for each system and decade from Brazil 2006, and a high year when at least three systems qualify. Compute each record's age-34 ten-year gap indicator centered across the 31 possible window starts, then subtract the fixed reference mean. Keep +5 percentage points as the smallest effect of interest. A confidence interval lying wholly below or wholly above +5 excludes that size or supports an effect above it for the new frame. Touching or crossing +5 leaves the decision unresolved. An age or cohort search, if undertaken, needs its own preregistered uncertainty adjustment.

If the reviewed sources fail admission, record the precise blockers and reopening evidence and leave the external effect unmeasured. Report metadata, aggregate counts and model results only to the extent each is documented and permitted. Retain the bilingual release and independent arithmetic review.
