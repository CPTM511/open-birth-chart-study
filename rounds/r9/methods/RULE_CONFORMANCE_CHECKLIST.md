# Rule conformance requirements for a later version

R9 does not revise the R8 engines. The following work is a future-model checklist, not a claim that it has been completed.

| System | Material boundary already disclosed in R8 | Required evidence for a later version |
|---|---|---|
| Bazi | Strength, pattern hierarchy, effective useful element, transformation and rescue adjudication remain incomplete | Source passages and executable premises; explicit ordering where patterns compete; positive and counterexamples; an unknown output where no unique judgment follows |
| Ziwei | Not all pattern/rescue priorities or qualitative thresholds are adjudicated | Star-table source comparison; declared cycle and age conventions; explicit rescue interactions and ambiguous cases; independently supplied chart fixtures |
| Qizheng | Modern mean-apogee Yuebo approximates the historical algorithm; precise Ming degree and degree-dependent periods remain absent | Historical formula reconstruction against almanac observations; separation of mean/osculating/historical definitions; boundary examples for degree-dependent periods; quantified disagreement rather than relabeling an approximation |
| Western | A selected geometric contact index is not a complete doctrine or adverse-event model | Named tradition and conventions; explicit dignity/sect/mitigation rules if added; transit/progression boundary examples; an observed-outcome model only in a separately authorized study |

For every proposed rule, require: (1) traceable original passage or documented algorithm; (2) inputs and prerequisites; (3) exact executable condition; (4) counterconditions and conflict priority; (5) positive and negative examples; (6) boundary and ambiguity examples; (7) a versioned implementation; (8) an evaluation plan fixed before inspecting new cohort effects. A different school is a distinct model version. It must not be selected because its cohort contrast looks more dramatic.

External expert agreement and empirical prediction are separate tests. Expert agreement can check whether a tradition has been implemented faithfully; it does not establish that the tradition predicts a real event. Likewise, astronomical agreement establishes positions under a named convention, not predictive validity.
