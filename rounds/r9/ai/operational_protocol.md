# Empirical AI track: operational protocol v0.1

Status: prospective design draft plus completed source acquisition/coverage audit. This is not a registered causal study or a completed forecast validation. No death-outcome study is included.

## Separate questions and units

| Question | Unit and candidate outcome | Evidence needed before a claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task automation | Versioned model × task × attempt: verified completion, reliability, human interventions, latency and cost | Stable task definitions, human baseline, tool permissions, held-out tasks, uncertainty and failure logs |
| Employment displacement | Reference area × occupation × time: employment, hours, earnings and, when available, entry into work | Comparable observed labor series, measured AI adoption/exposure, occupation crosswalk, valid controls and pre-treatment trends |
| Loss of control | Independently documented system/deployment episode: monitoring or intervention failure and recoverability | Demonstrated capability, deployment opportunity, harmful behavior and actual loss/recovery of control; laboratory and deployment cases separated |

The three endpoints are not interchangeable. Faster software-task completion is not an observation of job loss; job loss is not an observation of irreversible loss of control. The phrase “civilizational discontinuity” has no single measured endpoint in this release.

## Evidence admission rules

1. Preserve source identity, retrieval timestamp, release/version, schema, source hash and transformations. Distinguish provider estimates from our calculations.
2. Prefer official statistical records, original experiments, incident investigations and regulator/court records. Reports by a developer and independently replicated results receive separate labels.
3. Keep observed values separate from projections and modeled estimates. A blank observation-status field does not by itself certify a raw observation.
4. Predefine inclusion rules, outcome, time window, exclusions, minimum meaningful effect and multiplicity correction before examining the outcome contrast. Preserve deviations with reasons.
5. Report estimates and uncertainty. Do not turn non-significance into evidence of equivalence, nor an unusual pattern into a causal AI finding.
6. Birth-chart scores and astrological model agreement are excluded from empirical AI probability estimates. A linkage would require a separately specified, prospectively validated measurement theory; none exists in this project.

## Labor panel gate

The present annual occupation file is an availability audit only. It fails readiness for the proposed youth/cohort causal study because age is absent, occupation groups are broad, and adoption/treatment is absent.

Before fitting any causal model:

- Resolve reference areas versus regional aggregates; never count overlapping aggregates as independent countries.
- Choose one documented source and definition per area/period or explicitly model source transitions. Do not pool overlapping estimates.
- Adjudicate series breaks, unreliable flags, missing values, population definitions and ISCO changes. Preserve missing values; do not replace absent categories with zeros without evidence.
- Establish an occupation crosswalk and pre-treatment exposure definition without using later outcomes to choose it.
- Obtain adoption timing/intensity and relevant confounders. Potential task exposure alone is not adoption.
- Assess pre-trends, common shocks, selection into adoption and spillovers. Only use difference-in-differences/event studies if their assumptions have a defensible case; otherwise report descriptive associations.
- Predefine placebo dates, unaffected outcomes/groups, alternative source/occupation definitions and out-of-sample replication. A favorable post-hoc subgroup is exploratory.

No employment outcome has been selected as confirming AI displacement. No minimum meaningful effect or forecast threshold is fixed yet; those require the final, comparable frame. Avoid presenting a design target as an achieved result.

## Capability and control gate

Record benchmark version, task coverage, scaffolding, budgets and reliability. Revisions to the task suite or human-baseline procedure can change a time-horizon series. Retain provider confidence intervals and separate benchmark estimates from real deployment performance. No unbounded extrapolation is an admissible observed result.

For safety episodes, distinguish actual harm from potential hazards; deduplicate reporting and seek original records. Without exposure denominators, report counts as counts, not rates. Control-loss claims require a demonstrated failure of effective human intervention and a defined recovery criterion; a single erroneous output is insufficient.

## Deliverables and stop conditions

- Current: six-item evidence ledger, source manifest, METR numerical extract, labor subset and data-quality audit.
- Next admissible stage: a comparable labor frame with an auditable crosswalk, or a version-controlled capability/incident evidence update.
- Stop causal interpretation when adoption, controls or comparability remain unavailable. Publish the missing-data result.
- No chart-based AI risk probability, mortality prediction, global-representativeness claim, or claimed external peer review is authorized by these outputs.
