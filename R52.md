## Study specification for the custodian

The primary target is singleton live births actually occurring in 2009 at HA maternity units whose coverage can be established. A Zhejiang alternative may use a verified 1999 or 2009 frame. The study concerns concentrations in four specified birth-chart models. Its endpoint uses birth metadata and model calculations, with an estimate at the cohort level.

| Requirement | Minimal evidence or approved use |
| --- | --- |
| Record and fields | One row per live-born infant in the defined frame. Keep actual local birth date and observed hour and minute, recorded infant sex, and actual birth occurrence facility or reproducibly locatable place together. Use an internal key or custodian deduplication to rule out repeated records. |
| Frame and measurement | Before scoring, confirm the period, institutions, complete eligible counts, duplicate handling, missing clocks, default times, rounding, and the difference between the actual birth site and a reporting or certificate-issuing site. Freeze the geographic mapping, timezone and sampling plan. |
| Analysis | Apply the frozen four-system computation at ages 20–59 and the R50 P75 cutoffs from Brazil 2006. A ten-year gap has no year reaching the cutoff in at least three systems. Compare the record-centered gap for ages 34–43 to the fixed reference at the same start. Select the sample and uncertainty method from documented coverage and attainable precision before inspecting scores. |
| Review and publication | Provide controlled access to the required calculation and, if approved, an effect estimate and uncertainty, eligible counts and broad quality checks. R50 shows that the stratum counts and centered score moments suffice for a stratified simple random sample. Publication requires approved disclosure of the relevant aggregate evidence. [R50 arithmetic and replay](R50.md#verified-computation-for-a-controlled-archive) |

Once an admissible frame and written permission are confirmed, publish a separate cohort registration before scoring. An interval wholly below +5 percentage points excludes that size for the defined frame. An interval wholly above +5 supports an effect larger than the threshold. Touching or crossing the threshold leaves the result unresolved. [R51 registration](rounds/r51/REGISTRATION.md) · [Fixed reference and decision scale](rounds/r50/reference.json)

## Rehearsal for controlled computation

The rehearsal runs **12 fictional input records** through the existing R10 birth-only interface and the same four-model scoring worker used by R49. An independent calculation checked **1,920** threshold classifications and **372** centered windows. Across all **70** draws in a toy stratified sampling design, the exact sampling variance of the estimates matched the mean variance calculated from the stratum summaries. Two fresh processes in the reported environment produced identical score hashes. [Rehearsal code](rounds/r52/rehearse.py) · [Verification record](rounds/r52/rehearsal.json)

With the dependencies and timezone data described in the R49 guide, run `python -m rounds.r52.rehearse`. The command takes no archive inputs or prior score files.
