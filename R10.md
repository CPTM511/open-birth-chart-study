# R10 — independent four-system replay · 四体系独立复算

**27 November 2026.** The original 12,000 selected birth records produced 1,920,000 four-system scores across completed ages 20–59. The fixed Brazil 2006 reference had a 3.214943% joint high-score rate. Brazil 2020, Brazil 2024 and Mexico 2020 exceeded this reference by 0.615, 0.514 and 0.399 percentage points. The seven-slot adjusted intervals for these three contrasts lie above zero. The other three adjusted intervals include zero. All six upper bounds fall below the registered +1 percentage point resolution target.

巴西 2020、巴西 2024 和墨西哥 2020 三组的联合高分年度比例高于固定参照。六组的原评分输入和权重均已核验。**墨西哥两份历史抽样说明未找回**，其逐条设计资料由官方登记重新复原并单独封存。本轮按预先公开登记的独立复算发布。结论限于指定出生队列及四套模型生成的年龄轨迹。
