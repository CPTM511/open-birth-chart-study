## Verified computation for a controlled archive

Keep the R49 scores, ages 20–59, four age bands, and Brazil 2006 calibration fixed. For every system and band, the ordered reference scores yield one boundary and one exact comparison, either `>` or `>=`. At a repeated score, use its weighted midrank to decide whether the boundary itself qualifies. Compare the original score in binary64 at full precision. The reference file publishes **16** comparisons and **31** exact age-specific reference offsets. [Fixed reference](rounds/r50/reference.json) · [Comparison code](rounds/r50/compare.py)

For each record, let `A(a)` equal 1 when none of the ten years starting at age `a` reaches P75 in at least three systems. Use the integer `x = 31 × A(34) − sum(A(20), …, A(50))`. For simple random sampling without replacement within each stratum, the eligible count `N`, sampled count `n`, sum of `x` and sum of squares of `x` recover the design-weighted cohort mean and its finite-population sampling variance. Subtract the fixed age-34 reference offset from the mean. For small cells, retain the strata in the controlled calculation and release only approved overall summaries. The code requires two sampled records per noncensus stratum to estimate its variance.

The comparison agreed with the separate exact-rank and bit-window calculations on **3,746,560** flags and **725,896** centered windows from all **23,416** sealed reference and R49 records. All **48** tests at or next to a boundary agreed. All four R49 age-34 effects and standard errors reproduced within **5 × 10⁻¹²** on the proportion scale. The variance formula also matched the exact variance over **18** possible draws in an enumerated example. [Machine review](rounds/r50/review.json) · [Replay code](rounds/r50/verify.py)

Rebuild both evidence files using an authorized sealed R49 run and the R10 reference. Use a full clone and the environment in the R49 guide. [Prior-run reproduction](rounds/r49/REPRODUCE.md)

## Conditions for a new external test

Reopen when a custodian documents an authorized contemporaneous birth frame with exact local date and minute, recorded infant sex and a locatable birth occurrence on the same record. The preferred years are 1999 and 2009. Establish live-birth and multiple-delivery handling, timezone and geocoding rules, coverage by time and place, incomplete-record counts, frame disjointness and sampling strata before scoring. A hospital sample supports an inference for its defined frame. Freeze the cohort, uncertainty method and informative precision using the eligible counts and a documented variance assumption. As an illustration for design only, a two-sided 95% normal interval would need a planned standard error of about **0.71 percentage points** to have an 80% chance of falling wholly to one side of +5 if the true effect were +3 or +7 points.

Publish the effect for the ten-year window starting at age 34 relative to the fixed reference. An interval wholly below +5 excludes that size for the new frame. An interval wholly above +5 supports an effect above the threshold. Intervals touching or crossing +5 remain unresolved. Any later search over ages or cohorts needs its own registered adjustment.
